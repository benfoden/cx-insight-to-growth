Product brief · Fernhill Supply Co. · Jul 27 to Sep 20, 2026 · Sources: GA4 (219,152 sessions), 150 support tickets, support team notes

Key findings

1

The shipping fee appears too late in checkout

Payment step-through fell from 68% to 56% after Aug 24, while shipping cost tickets rose from 8 to 25.

Next step: Show the estimated shipping cost in the cart, before checkout starts.

2

Promo codes fail on iPhone Safari

16 tickets report a code that did not apply, 94% from mobile. Agents now apply discounts by hand.

Next step: Fix the promo code field on iOS Safari and test it before each email send.

3

Product pages miss the facts that shoppers ask for

Size (18), waterproof (13) and PFAS (11) questions make up 42 tickets, all before purchase.

Next step: Add chest and sleeve sizes, the waterproof rating and the coating type to product pages.
