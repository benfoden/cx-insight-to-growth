The free-shipping banner makes a promise checkout breaks, and Paid Social rarely converts

Marketing brief · Fernhill Supply Co. · Jul 27 to Sep 20, 2026 · Sources: GA4 (219,152 sessions), 150 support tickets, support team notes

Key findings

1

The banner says free shipping but does not say the minimum

Shipping cost tickets rose from 8 to 25, and 46% of “free shipping” searches end the visit.

Next step: Add “on orders over $99” to every banner and ad that says free shipping.

2

Paid Social brings traffic that does not buy

Paid Social has 23% of sessions but converts at 0.5%, against 1.9% for the site.

Next step: Move part of the Paid Social budget to Email and Paid Search until the landing page is fixed.

3

Email codes fail on mobile and cost sales

16 tickets say a promo code did not work, 94% of them on mobile.

Next step: Send auto-apply discount links in email, not codes, until the bug is fixed.
